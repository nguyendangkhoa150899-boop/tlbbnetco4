NETCO4 - BAN SUA CHO CLIENT DA TAI
==================================

Dung khi ban da tai client tu truoc (ban cu: toan man hinh / chua vao duoc NetCo4).
Neu tai NetCo4-Client.zip moi thi KHONG can goi nay.

1. Giai nen tat ca file trong goi nay VAO THU MUC GAME
   (thu muc co san Bin, Data, Patch, Accounts). Chon "Replace/Ghi de" neu duoc hoi.
2. Chay CAI-DAT-FIX.cmd. Windows hoi quyen admin thi bam Yes.
3. Mo game bang NetCo4.cmd.

Goi nay lam:
- Tro ve server NetCo4 (103.216.118.123)
- Choi che do cua so 1280x720 (khong full man hinh)
- Tat tu cap nhat tu web server cu
- Chan link la cua nut Nap the / Dang ky
- Xoa ten dang nhap cu da luu
